=== PAGE 1 ===
Autopilot to maintain movement of a drone in a vertical plane at a constant height
in the presence of vision-based navigation
Alexander Domoshnitsky1, Max Kogan1, Oleg Kupervaser1,2,Roman Yavich1
1 Department of Mathematics, Ariel University, Israel; 2 TRANSIST VIDEO LLC, Skolkovo,
Russia
Abstract
In this report we describe correct operation of autopilot for supply correct drone flight. There exists
noticeable delay in getting information about position and orientation of a drone to autopilot in the
presence of vision-based navigation. In spite of this fact, we demonstrate that it is possible to
provide stable flight at a constant height in a vertical plane. We describe how to form relevant
controlling signal for autopilot in the case of the navigation information delay.
