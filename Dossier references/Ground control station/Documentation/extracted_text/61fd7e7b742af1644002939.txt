COMPATIBILITY
The Integrated Ground Controller is directly compatible with air vehicles using Ardupilot and PX4 firmware. It
is generally compatible with any flight controller which uses PPM for R/C control and the MAVLINK messaging
protocol for telemetry. On the ground side, telemetry can be streamed to a number of ground station applications
such as Hitec Mission Control, Mission Planner, QGroundControl and Solex.
