1 Introduction
Inrecentyears,thefieldsofapplicationofaerialmanipulatorshaveexpanded,
allowing the manipulation of objects in flight [1][2] and the interaction with the
environment [3]. Some application examples are the inspection of oil and gas
refineries, chemical plants [4], power lines [5], or civil infrastructures [6,7]. In
order to monitor these missions, a GCS (Ground Control Station) is necessary.
Ground Control Stations have an important role in the aerial manipulation, as
theyareusedforplanningmissions,monitoringthestatusoftheaerialplatform
orestablishingcommunicationlinks.SomegoodexamplesofGCSareQGround-
Control [8], Mission Planner [9] or MAVProxy [10].
However, these GCS are primarily intended for the flight of aerial vehicles,
without taking into account the robotic manipulator. In addition, most of them
are designed for outdoor flight, using GPS. This makes it difficult to integrate
an indoor positioning system such as OptiTrack. Furthermore, waypoints are

3 Implementation of the Ground Control Station
TheGroundControlStationhasbeendevelopedcompletelyinPython,using
Tkinter forthedesignoftheGUI(GraphicalUserInterface),andPymavlink for
the implementation of the MAVLink protocol.
3.1 Threads
Thesoftwareconsistsoffivemainthreads,plusauxiliarythreads,runningin
parallel. Figure 4 summarizes the main threads, and their inputs and outputs.
1) GUI thread. It is responsible for updating the content on the screen, as
well as responding to button presses.
2)MAVLinkreceptionthread. Thisthreadreceivesthemessagessenttothe
GCS by the aerial manipulator using MAVLink protocol. It also processes them
and generates the corresponding actions. It continuously checks the connection
status, displaying on screen if the connection with the aerial platform has been
lost.

3.2 Communications
The GCS establishes a connection between the different devices. This con-
nectioniseitherwireless,aswiththeaerialmanipulator,orbycableiftheother
deviceisclosetothegroundcomputer,likewiththegroundmanipulatorusedin
theslavemode.Figure5showsageneralschemeoftheconnectionstotheGCS.
The connection between the autopilot and the GRVC GCS is established us-
ing the MAVLink protocol. MAVLink has been configured to operate over UDP
protocol. In this way, fast data transmission is achieved, although with lower
reliability than the TCP protocol. In the same way, the connection between the
GRVC GCS and other GCSs is established via MAVLink over UDP.

The aerial manipulator used for the experimental validation of the GCS is

The aerial platform is composed of a DJI F550 frame, which includes the

and the XRotor 40A ESCs. The autopilot selected for the aerial platform is

5 Experiments
The experimental validation of the GCS is done through three different ex-
periments. First, the integration of OptiTrack system is tested by performing a
defined mission with waypoints in Section 5.1. Then, the two predefined move-
ments of the aerial manipulator are validated in Section 5.2. Finally, Section 5.3
validates the slave mode by a capture operation of an object.
All the experiments are carried out in the GRVC indoor facilities, which is
equipped with the OptiTrack positioning system composed of 28 cameras. The
videos of the experiments are available in [11].

6 Conclusion
This paper has presented the development and validation of a Ground Con-
trol Station for Aerial Manipulators. The objective of this GCS is to integrate
the information of the manipulator and the aerial platform in only one GUI.
The paper has presented a functional design that allows the integration of the
OptiTrack positioning system, the sending of messages to the autopilot and the
simple definition of waypoints. The solution presented also allows the control
of the aerial manipulator in different modes (sending joints coordinates, with
predefined movements or slave mode) and the video streaming to facilitate the
manipulation tasks. All the functionalities of the GRVC GCS have been tested
in indoor tests with an aerial manipulator.
